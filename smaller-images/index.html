FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does SmallerImage upload my photos? +
Never. All processing happens on your Mac's CPU/GPU — the app doesn't even have network access. It passes Apple's sandbox and hardened runtime verification.
Which formats can I convert between? +
HEIC, JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, and GIF — all directions. Animated GIF frames are preserved.
How is this different from Preview or online compressors? +
Preview can't batch, and online tools upload your images to a stranger's server. SmallerImage gives you batch processing, reusable presets, format conversion and a visual comparison — all offline.
Will my image quality suffer? +
That's exactly what the Compare view is for. Pinch-zoom to pixel level, drag the slider, and if you're not happy, tap "Redo" with different settings. Pick quality 100% for lossless re-encoding.
Is it a one-time purchase or subscription? +
Both. Free tier covers light use. Pro is subscription (monthly/yearly) or a Lifetime buyout. All on the Mac App Store — no credit card passed to us.
What Mac do I need? +
macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Universal binary — optimized for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and still runs on Intel.
Which languages are supported? +
English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, and French. The app auto-follows your macOS language setting.
