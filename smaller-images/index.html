FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does SmallerImage upload my photos? +
No. SmallerImage processes image pixels and metadata on your Mac. It does not upload images or use analytics and advertising SDKs. StoreKit connects to Apple when you buy or restore Pro.
Which formats can I convert between? +
HEIC, JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, AVIF, and GIF are supported as inputs. Export to JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, TIFF, or AVIF. GIF conversion creates a still image; animated frames are not preserved.
Can I resize without cropping, or crop to an exact size? +
Both. Use ≤ Max to fit the complete image inside a width and height while preserving its aspect ratio. Use = Equal (crop) to fill exact dimensions and automatically center-crop the excess. SmallerImage does not provide free-form crop positioning.
How is this different from Preview or online compressors? +
SmallerImage combines target file size, dimensions and format in one batch. You can compare each result and use Delivery Pack to create several organized output versions in one run.
Will my image quality suffer? +
Use Compare to inspect detail, gradients and transparency before export. The five-step PNG FAST–BEST slider trades time for more thorough palette optimization; it reduces colors and can change image detail or transparency. Higher levels are not guaranteed to improve every image. For lossy formats such as JPEG, even a 100% quality setting is not guaranteed to be mathematically lossless.
Is the target file size guaranteed? +
Target size is best effort. Outputs above the limit are flagged. PNG may also need smaller pixel dimensions after reducing colors, so check the actual size and dimensions before export. For photos, try JPG, WebP or HEIC, reduce dimensions, or increase the limit.
Is it a one-time purchase or subscription? +
Both. The free tier supports up to five images per batch with manual controls. Pro is $1.99/month, $9.99/year, or a $19.99 lifetime purchase. Final regional pricing is shown by the Mac App Store before purchase.
What Mac do I need? +
macOS 14 Sonoma or later. The universal binary is optimized for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and also runs on Intel.
Which languages are supported? +
English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, and French. The app auto-follows your macOS language setting.
