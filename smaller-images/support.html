Support

Updated August 20, 2026

Answers to common questions about compression, Free and Pro access, exports, and purchases.

How many images can I compress for free?

There is no daily limit. The free version processes up to five images per batch with all formats and manual compression controls. Presets require Pro.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro removes the five-image batch limit and unlocks all built-in and custom presets. It is available for $1.99/month, $9.99/year, or as a $19.99 lifetime purchase. The Mac App Store shows the final price for your region before purchase. Family Sharing is not included.

What formats are supported?

SmallerImage supports common formats including PNG, JPEG, HEIC, WebP, TIFF, AVIF, and GIF as inputs. Animated GIF frames are not preserved when exporting to a still-image format.

Where are my compressed images saved?

Choose a folder, save beside each source image, or export individual results. When replacing source files, keep a backup because lossy compression cannot be undone.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image processing runs locally on your Mac. SmallerImage does not upload your images or use analytics or advertising SDKs.

How do I restore or manage a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases in the Pro window. Manage or cancel subscriptions in macOS System Settings under Apple ID and Subscriptions.
