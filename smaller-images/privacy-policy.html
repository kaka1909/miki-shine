Legal / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

SmallerImage processes your images on your Mac. The app does not upload images, run analytics, show ads, or send usage data to the developer.

Image processing

SmallerImage reads the images you select and writes compressed or converted files to locations you choose. The app performs this work on your Mac. MikiShine does not receive your images or image metadata.

Data stored on your Mac

The app stores the following information in its local app container:

  • Your presets, compression settings, language choice, and export preferences.
  • An encrypted daily compression count used to enforce the free limit.
  • A random encryption key in macOS Keychain. The app generates this key and does not derive it from hardware or another device identifier.

SmallerImage does not send this information to MikiShine. You can remove container data by deleting the app and its Application Support data. You can delete exported images in Finder.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and lifetime purchases through the App Store. SmallerImage reads your current StoreKit entitlement so it can unlock Pro features. MikiShine does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and third-party services

The macOS app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK. It does not create an account or contact a MikiShine server.

Support email

If you email support, MikiShine receives the address, message, and attachments you choose to send. MikiShine uses that information to answer your request and keeps it only as long as needed for support and legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the address below.

Children

SmallerImage does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's local image processing works the same for every user.

Policy changes

MikiShine may update this policy when the app's data practices change. This page shows the effective date of the current version.
